# Record the post-rc.2 boundary and close the wave stories

## Goal

Bring the tracking ledgers back into agreement with the tree. Thirty-two stories are implemented on
`main` with their final row deferred to a shared push boundary, and the board's generated region
still lists thirteen of them as `ready`. Write the post-`rc.2` changelog, close every story whose
acceptance now holds, and make the board, roadmap and maturity report say the same thing.

## Acceptance

- [x] `CHANGELOG.md` `[Unreleased]` describes the complete post-`rc.2` boundary grouped by the
      capability delivered rather than by story ID: the fourteen external-review findings, bounded
      endpoint resolution, the call-module split, the shipped-verb guides, G.722, and the speech and
      call-audio-analysis contracts. Migration guidance, fixed defects and measurement limits are
      stated the way the `rc.2` entry states them.
- [x] Every story whose acceptance rows all hold is closed with `/track:done`. A story with a
      genuinely open row keeps `in-progress` and its Progress log states exactly what remains —
      `A-10` (stable publication) is expected to stay open and is not closed by this wave.
- [x] `S-49`, `S-50` and `S-51` close without further implementation: their acceptance carries no open
      row today and their outcome already shipped in `rc.2`.
- [x] `A-37` and `P-14` record the `v1.0.0-rc.2` publication evidence their acceptance requires —
      registry checksums, workflow run, tag object, release commit, release URL and the five-target
      portable asset set — and then close against it.
- [x] The board's generated region is regenerated by `/track:board` and contains no story whose
      rendered status disagrees with its frontmatter. The hand-written Status section names the
      delivered wave and the current release boundary.
- [x] `docs/roadmap.md` status, the milestone sections this wave closes, and `maturity.py`'s predicate
      tables agree with the closed set. No alpha or v1 predicate is marked met without a story
      declaring it.
- [x] Depends on `X-112`. No story closes against a gate that has not been run green.

## Progress

- 2026-08-08: selected as the ledger half of the release-boundary wave. Measured state at selection:
  35 stories `in-progress`, 0 `ready`, 2 `blocked`; 29 of the 35 hold exactly one open acceptance row,
  and three (`S-49`, `S-50`, `S-51`) hold none.

- 2026-08-08: **the ledger agrees with the tree.** `CHANGELOG.md` carries the complete post-`rc.2`
  boundary as `[1.0.0-rc.3]`. Thirty-one stories closed, including `S-49`, `S-50`, `S-51` and `M-44`,
  which had no open acceptance row at all, and `P-14`, whose five-target artifact evidence the
  `v1.0.0-rc.2` tag run supplied. `A-37` closed against recorded registry checksums, tag object,
  release commit, workflow run and release URL. `A-10`, `M-57`, `M-70` and `T-39` stay open with
  their remaining work stated rather than being closed to make the count tidy. The board's generated
  region and `docs/maturity.md` were regenerated after the last status moved, and 17 stories that
  quoted their `pillar` were normalized — `maturity.py` groups by the raw value, so `"Phone"` and
  `Phone` had been reported as two pillars.
