# Record the post-rc.2 boundary and close the wave stories

## Goal

Bring the tracking ledgers back into agreement with the tree. Thirty-two stories are implemented on
`main` with their final row deferred to a shared push boundary, and the board's generated region
still lists thirteen of them as `ready`. Write the post-`rc.2` changelog, close every story whose
acceptance now holds, and make the board, roadmap and maturity report say the same thing.

## Acceptance

- [ ] `CHANGELOG.md` `[Unreleased]` describes the complete post-`rc.2` boundary grouped by the
      capability delivered rather than by story ID: the fourteen external-review findings, bounded
      endpoint resolution, the call-module split, the shipped-verb guides, G.722, and the speech and
      call-audio-analysis contracts. Migration guidance, fixed defects and measurement limits are
      stated the way the `rc.2` entry states them.
- [ ] Every story whose acceptance rows all hold is closed with `/track:done`. A story with a
      genuinely open row keeps `in-progress` and its Progress log states exactly what remains —
      `A-10` (stable publication) is expected to stay open and is not closed by this wave.
- [ ] `S-49`, `S-50` and `S-51` close without further implementation: their acceptance carries no open
      row today and their outcome already shipped in `rc.2`.
- [ ] `A-37` and `P-14` record the `v1.0.0-rc.2` publication evidence their acceptance requires —
      registry checksums, workflow run, tag object, release commit, release URL and the five-target
      portable asset set — and then close against it.
- [ ] The board's generated region is regenerated by `/track:board` and contains no story whose
      rendered status disagrees with its frontmatter. The hand-written Status section names the
      delivered wave and the current release boundary.
- [ ] `docs/roadmap.md` status, the milestone sections this wave closes, and `maturity.py`'s predicate
      tables agree with the closed set. No alpha or v1 predicate is marked met without a story
      declaring it.
- [ ] Depends on `X-112`. No story closes against a gate that has not been run green.

## Progress

  35 stories `in-progress`, 0 `ready`, 2 `blocked`; 29 of the 35 hold exactly one open acceptance row,
  and three (`S-49`, `S-50`, `S-51`) hold none.
